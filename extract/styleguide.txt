INTRODUCTION
The following guidelines have
been developed to assist designers
with the successful application of
the Racing Victoria masterbrand.
Maintaining consistency in all forms
of communication is paramount to
the success of the brand.

8
Clear space is designed to
assist in maintaining the
clarity and integrity of the
Racing Victoria masterbrand.
Clear space is the minimum
surface area surrounding
the masterbrand that must
remain free of any other
graphic element or text.
Wherever possible, maintain
more clear space around the
symbol than the minimum
specified.
Minimum size specifications
are provided to ensure the
masterbrand is reproduced
effectively at a small size. A
width of 40mm (horizontal
mark) and 18mm (vertical
mark) has been determined
as the minimum sizings
at which the masterbrand
reproduces effectively
in general methods of
production – i.e. print.

9
The Racing Victoria
masterbrand may not be
redrawn or altered in any way,
under any circumstances.
The Racing Victoria
masterbrand may only be
reproduced in the ways shown
throughout these guidelines.
These examples show various
ways in which the Racing
Victoria masterbrand might
be incorrectly reproduced.
INCORRECT USAGE
Do not compress, extend or
distort the Racing Victoria
masterbrand.
Do not adjust the spacing
between the Racing Victoria
masterbrand letterforms.
Do not reproduce the Racing
Victoria masterbrand in
alternative typefaces.
Do not alter the colour of
the Racing Victoria masterbrand.
Do not place complex imagery
behind the Racing Victoria
masterbrand.
Do not use effects such as drop
shadows on the Racing Victoria
masterbrand.
Do not infringe upon the
clearspace of the Racing Victoria
masterbrand.
Do not rotate the Racing
Victoria masterbrand.

The Racing Victoria
masterbrand colour palette
is a key element in creating
consistent and recognisable
communications.
The primary colours used
in the masterbrand are
blue and magenta as well as
grey. A gradient has been
developed from the blue and
magenta and is used within
the primary masterbrand.
As secondary colour palette
has been developed also to
provide flexibility within all
forms of communication.
When preparing artwork
for online communications
please use the RGB and hex
colour values.
