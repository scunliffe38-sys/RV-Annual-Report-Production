What I need to build the FY26 report
Sections, inputs and required formats

Derived from the FY25 InDesign file: 84 pages, 43 spreads, 31 tables. Sections below are grouped as they run in the printed book. FY26 page numbers will shift, so page ranges are FY25's and indicative only.

One rule underpins everything: send raw values, not presentation. Every rounding, percentage, variance and total is calculated at build time. If a figure is calculated in the source file, send the inputs instead and I will do the arithmetic once, consistently, in one place.

The workbook contract

Applies to every spreadsheet input. The FY25 workbooks broke three of these, which is what caused the missing participation figures.

Rule Why
One metric per row, one period per column Adding FY27 becomes a new column, not a new layout
Labels in column A, stable year to year Labels are the key I match on. Rewording one silently drops a row
Unrounded values, no thousands separators, no units in the cell “$7.61bn” is text. 7610000000 is a number I can round and total correctly
No merged cells, no blank spacer rows or columns Merges shift the column index and misread the whole sheet
No variance, total or percentage columns All 32 KPI variances are calculated. Supplied ones can only disagree
Empty cell for genuinely unavailable data Prints as N/A. A typed “N/A”, “-” or 0 each mean something different
.xlsx, one tab per table, tab names unchanged Renamed tabs are the most common reason a rebuild fails

Photography and imagery

Resolution and colour 300 dpi at final print size, CMYK, TIFF or maximum-quality JPEG. Full-bleed images need 3 mm bleed on every trimmed edge
Naming Section and sequence, matching the caption list: welfare-01.tif. Renaming after supply breaks the link
Captions and credits One tab: filename, caption, credit, usage clearance. FY25 carried an unresolved note about an image that could not be used — clearances need to be settled before supply, not during layout
Delivery OneDrive once your IT security team approves the connector. Until then everything except imagery can proceed

What I do not need

Pre-rounded or pre-formatted numbers. Variance, total or percentage columns. Charts as images — send the series and I will draw them. Copy laid out in InDesign or PowerPoint. Screenshots of tables. Anything already in one of the workbooks, sent a second time by another route: two sources for one figure is how they diverge.
